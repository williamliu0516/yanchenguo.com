PICA

A Parkour Escape Game Inside a Living Shell

PICA is a third-person parkour game inspired by pica, a condition involving the persistent eating of non-food substances. The game turns the idea of being drawn to a non-food object into a search for glowing fragments. The player collects fragments to unlock each level’s exit. In Levels 2 and 3, tentacles emerge from the shell walls as the player explores. The player wants to take the fragments. The shell wants to keep the player inside.
