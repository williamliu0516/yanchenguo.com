About

I make interactive installations and games, starting with things I want to understand better.

Hi, I’m Yanchen Guo, an artist and interaction designer based in New York, studying Art and Technology at Pratt Institute.

I started with drawing and making things by hand. Now I also work with code, sensors, and game engines. My projects often begin with a personal memory or something I come across while researching. Recent work explores childhood experiences, the effects of illness, and the habits people carry through their lives.

I enjoy figuring out how to turn an idea into something people can interact with. Sometimes that means building a moving object; other times, it means designing a space to explore in a game. Watching someone try the work is one of my favorite parts. They often approach it differently than I expected, and that gives me something to work on next.
